> [...] by holding up a mirror to their actions, you teach them a lesson.
> Robert Greene, 48 Laws of Power, Law 44

I started kickboxing at an MMA gym in October 2022. At first, I didn't make much progress. There was one guy, Ömer, who always sparred too hard, even after I asked him to go light with me. This really bothered me, and I wanted to teach him a lesson. 

The problem was that I was 15 kg lighter than him and I was also very unathletic, as I hadn't done any sport for 10 years before starting kickboxing.

I did not hate this guy, actually I liked him. Just his way of training and sparring really annoyed me. Since he did not react to my requests to go light, I wanted to teach him how it felt, when he was the weaker guy.
