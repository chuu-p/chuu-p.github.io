in the early days of my career after i got my bachelors degree my boss asked me about my wishes and preferences as a software developer. i told her, i am happy anytime i get to the actual coding. it was my favorite part of my job and as a junior i just wanted to have the most fun at my job and did not think about the future or my career. (s/o sonja)
it is kind of ironic looking back, the first big change i am witnessing in my career is the automation of the skill have been honing for the last 9 years. first, i thought this is no different than dock workers who got replaced by containers and cranes. they built their skill and strength over many years, which now was useless. now, your proficiency of operating a crane was what decided your productiveness and therefore also salary.

i am glad that this was my state of mind at the time, because would i enjoy every second of thinking through hard technical problems.

when i see places where i used to live, or maybe even just rode my bike through i still remember what music was playing and what i was thinking at the time. this is the same for source code i actually wrote myself.

> translating my thoughts into code

this was the part of the job that made it so creative for me
my narcissistic trait is that i like to do most things in which you can have a distinct style and express yourself with. this is the case for kickboxing and other martial arts, programming by hand, skating, playing the trumpet, etc. (and writing about myself in my blog and diary lol)

i have a sticker on my small server cabinet that reads:

> i am a pro. gram. mer. (i have an awesome life)

i also told everyone, that maybe i havent fount the ideal company, i definitely have found the right job, since programming was so fun.

if it were up to me i would use ai for the boring parts of my job like scrum ritual meetings, and filling out forms but not for the fun parts like programming and designing larger systems.

i was not able to produce sensational, groundbreaking software like widely used open source tools, but i cared about every line.

i am now pressured by the world to use ai to code, since programmers using it are seemingly more productive and therefore cheaper. i am worried about skill drain and i think my brain will rot when i dont challenge it enough. this is why i am now coding a lot more in my free time, to counteract this.

i want to track the commits and stuff for a user, but i dont want to host a git server on a phone, this is a distributed project, i cannot (only) use github, since i want to connect to my local git server too? idea: webhooks and the github or git server api

programming

1. agi utopia
2. ai enhancement
3. ai replacement
4. agi dystopia

i choose to believe it will be fine, since it is more convenient for me (like heiter)

kraft: i want someone to praise me at the end of my life

we have a duty to create agi to reduce suffering, even on the way

## predictions

long term vibecoding is not part of any bench mark or similar
if ai coding works like evolution
99.9 % of organismns fail!
long term vibecoding will accumulate more and more tech debt
this will not be maintainable even with ai

i love talking about my favorite programming language. and talking about programming language syntax in general. imagine rust, but as a scripting language. in general discussing for loops vs map functions and comparing compiled languages to interpreted ones and the respective developer experience. i think this will be lost in the vibe coding era. programmers will loose their spark and passion for programming.

> my job will also become easier, right?

every software product has to get a lot better now, right?
logically thinking every product has to get a lot better very fast since new features are so cheap and quick to implement
why this could maybe not be the case :
ais cannot train on ai generated material. if they do, their performance decreases. so if more and more code gets generated by ai, the performance of current and future models could decrease because of the artificial data in the training data.
also context rot is a problem right now

> boats are safe in the haven, but this is not what their made for

i hope ai will **not** become the haven. i want **my** ship to sail. if you want to travel the sea, choose people who long for the ocean. i hope this does not get lost in the transition to **vibe** coding.

> no glory in easy battles

this is why programmers get burned out when using ai

## humans are built different

at the end of the day this is just caused by capitalism. moloch is unstoppable, and this could be the disease to kill this emergent being born from the combined greed of all human minds, which are themselves just shaped though evolution and the principle of resource accumulation. ai will not have these barbaric naturalistic instincts, which could be a good sign for the future.

> i want to believe

because of my life philosophy, i have to be pro ai and pro agi. i believe in dont die and want to never die. i want to live for thousands, millions, billions of years if possible.
i dont think humans will solve the problem of aging in my lifetime. but i think agi can. the problem with this view is that, if an ai can solve aging but humans cant, the ai has to be smarter than humanity. if it is smarter than humanity, it can probably improve itself, take over the world and do what it wants. since the nature of an agi is unknowable and every take on this is speculative, i believe a smarter being will develop better ethics than humans and agi will bring forth an utopia, out of convenience. it is nicer for my life to have this view, than to be a doomer.

> we need agi to extend our lives but when she does, she also takes over the world
