## #dev/project/blog/post on focus

- How to learn optimally
	- you need to be a surgeon when reading books, only reading exactly the part u need
	- https://every.to/superorganizers/surgical-reading-how-to-read-12-books-580014

- building muscle / bulking
	- It is not possible to suddenly eat double the amount you ate previously. It is better to change what you eat, not increase the volume blindly. If you want to put on more muscle, changing some of what you eat to contain more protein rich sources, while keeping calories and other macros stable, is the best way I made progress

- some players in league of legends play for 10.000 hours but are still stuck in lower ranks. Some people play just for half a year and reach high ranks. I was also stuck at lower ranks, until I realized what I needed to change with my gameplay, and I had a breakthrough after that
- Also, in kickboxing i took over many people in a year of training in skill, but then there was a 17 year old kid who beat me after training just for half a year.

This principle applies to any skill development endeavor

**not** practice makes perfect but **perfect practice** makes perfect

you need to identify the necessary steps to make the most progress

because you only have a limited amount of energy!!!

Going to the gym taught me one thing. I only have a limited amount of energy. If I start my workout by running for an hour straight, I don't have energy to do the exercises that actually stimulate muscle growth. If I power through it all, I will take way longer to recover, than if I just did the right exercises optimally. This is the best way to go, to make the most progress in the least amount of time.
